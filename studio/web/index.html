A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Bring your target voice into focus.

  1. Enable devices

    Allow camera and microphone access. Keep your mouth clearly visible in the frame.

  2. Follow the mouth automatically

    Auto tracking detects the face and mouth on this Mac. The green outline follows the lips. If the face is briefly lost, separation continues with the last crop and follows the face again when it returns. Select Reacquire when the target changes. You can also choose Manual crop; dragging the box or using arrow keys switches to manual mode.

  3. Load a model and start separation

    Each update uses the latest 2 seconds of audio and video to produce a 0.5-second output. The first result needs time to build context. Older windows are skipped when inference cannot keep pace.

  4. Listen, then save

    Put on headphones to enable live monitoring. After stopping, listen to or download the model output. Recordings retain up to 5 minutes; live separation and meeting output continue until you stop them.

Connect to Zoom / Tencent Meeting

Choose a physical microphone as Falcon’s input. Select BlackHole 2ch as the meeting output and enable it. In your meeting app, choose BlackHole 2ch as the microphone and your headphones as the speakers. Keep system audio routed to headphones so other participants’ voices do not feed back into the microphone.

Install BlackHole or another virtual audio device and use Chrome, which supports choosing an output device. Chrome also supports background video capture, so you can switch to your meeting window. If another browser uses foreground capture, keep Falcon visible; hiding the page stops capture. Meetings receive completed separation segments, so slower devices may introduce delays or gaps.

The visible speaker must be synchronized with the microphone audio. Results depend on lighting, framing, background noise, and the model’s training data.
