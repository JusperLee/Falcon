A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Bring your target voice into focus.

  1. Enable devices

    Allow camera and microphone access. Keep your mouth clearly visible in the frame.

  2. Follow the mouth automatically

    Auto tracking detects the face and mouth in your browser. The green outline follows the lips. If the face is briefly lost, the last crop is kept until the face returns. Select Reacquire when the target changes, or choose Manual crop; dragging the box or using arrow keys switches to manual mode.

  3. Record, then separate

    Select Start recording, speak or play some sound, then select Finish and separate. Up to 60 seconds; the recording and its mouth crops are uploaded and separated on a GPU with the live studio’s windows (2 s of context, 0.5 s hop).

  4. Listen, then save

    After separation, switch between Separated and Mixture to compare, and export WAV. The Space stores no audio or video; results stay in your browser.

Need live separation?

ZeroGPU allocates a GPU for each call and counts it against a daily quota, which does not suit a loop that runs every 0.5 s. For live separation and meeting output, run Falcon Studio locally (studio/ in the GitHub repository).

Falcon Studio on GitHub ↗

ZeroGPU gives each Hugging Face account a daily GPU quota, with more quota when signed in. The visible speaker must be synchronized with the microphone audio. Results depend on lighting, framing, background noise, and the model’s training data.
